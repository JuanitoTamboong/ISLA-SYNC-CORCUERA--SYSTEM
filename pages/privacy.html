ISLA-SYNC CORCUERA

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 24, 2026

Isla-Sync Corcuera (“we,” “us,” or “our”) provides a community information and reporting service for residents and visitors of Corcuera. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you use the Isla-Sync Corcuera mobile application and related pages.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the service, we may collect:

  • Account information: your name, email address, password credentials handled by our authentication provider, and account role.
  • Profile information: details you choose to add to your profile.
  • Reports and submissions: descriptions, photos or other files, dates, and related details that you submit about community concerns.
  • Location information: location details you choose to attach to a report or use with maps and directions.
  • Technical information: basic device, browser, app, and diagnostic information needed to operate, secure, and improve the service.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to create and secure accounts, provide community news and tourist information, receive and manage reports, show relevant map and direction features, communicate about submitted reports, maintain service security, prevent misuse, and improve the app.

3. When We Share Information

We may share information with authorized Corcuera personnel who need it to review and respond to reports, and with service providers that help us host, authenticate, store, or operate the app. We may also disclose information when required by law or when necessary to protect users, the public, or the service. We do not sell personal information.

Information included in a report may be visible to authorized administrators and may be used to coordinate a response. Avoid including sensitive information that is not necessary for the report.

4. Service Providers

We use Supabase for authentication and data services. We may also use map, directions, weather, hosting, and analytics-related services where those features are enabled. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies and only as needed to provide their services.

5. Retention and Security

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service, manage community records, resolve reports, meet legal obligations, and protect our rights. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Choices and Requests

You may review or update available profile information through the app. To request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information, contact us using the address below. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request. Deleting information may affect your ability to use account-based features and some records may be retained when legally required or necessary for public safety.

7. Children’s Privacy

The service is intended for a general community audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information so we can review and remove it where appropriate.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the service or legal requirements change. We will post the revised policy on this page and update the effective date.
